## Finance rules and exact gaps

### Invoice document and receivable

Current document states are DRAFT, APPROVED, ISSUED and CANCELLED. Snapshot and number are protected after issue. PDF retry uses the existing document. Keep document workflow separate from derived payment/settlement progress to preserve issued immutability. Add review, approved draft correction and reasoned cancellation, receivable aging, dispute/write-off controls and due/overdue work queues. Issuance creates an invoiced amount and receivable. It creates zero recognized sales. Credit notes are currently immutable positive adjustments limited to unpaid balance. Approved statutory numbering, immutable document/PDF, reversals/refunds and dispute policies still need design and owner review.

### Payments and sales recognition

- In server/finance.ts, PAYMENT writes a receipt with amount, currency, client, reference and date. ALLOCATE_PAYMENT can use a non-reversed receipt immediately if the invoice is issued and client/currency and balances match. REVERSE_PAYMENT preserves the receipt and subtracts its allocations from current payment sums. Existing idempotency protects retry with the same request key. It does not prove independent requests referencing the same bank transaction are duplicates.
- In app/workspace/page.tsx the dashboard labels issued invoice subtotals as issued sales and sums non-reversed payments as collections. server/reports.ts uses non-reversed allocations for balances and receipt dates for payment reporting. There is no verification distinction or recognition ledger. An unverified receipt can currently affect displayed balances. Do not infer verification from these totals or convert historical receipts into recognized sales.
- Add Admin verification/rejection with method, bank/reference, payer, proof, actor/time, comments and reason. Define approved unique reference scope and repeated cash/manual reference handling. Require verified receipt and same-client/currency issued invoice for confirmed allocation. Use minor units, transaction isolation and idempotent events.
- Owner sketch rule is issued invoice plus recorded payment plus Admin-confirmed funds. Pending receipts remain visible but never contribute to verified collections or recognition. Default proposed recognition is full verified settlement only, with partial recognition gated by owner approval. Maintain append-only recognition and compensating reversal linked to verified allocations and invoice. Historical as-of reports must use effective timestamps, not today's reversal flag. Approve treatment of tax, credits, disputes and period close before implementation.

### Expenses and controlled correction

Current Expense supports submission, approval/rejection and paid transition with category, date, description, amount, currency and state. It lacks drafts/review, proof, attribution, supplier, method, paid/approval actor and time, required reasons and correction/reversal history. Dashboard combines approved and paid expenses. Plan distinct review, approved, paid, rejected and reversed totals, attribution filters and controlled replacement or reversal.

Current finance audit hands the command to the audit helper as its after payload, with no before snapshot on that path. Receipt and issued invoice triggers protect key history. Add structured redacted before/after, actor role, date, reason and correlation on approval, rejection, verification, cancellation and reversal. Restrict draft editing/deletion by eligibility, dependencies and explicit reason. Prefer audited void where approved. Protect issued invoices, verified receipts, approved/paid expenses and operational records after custody or billing from physical deletion or silent edit. Dispute/write-off or refund handling needs explicit legal/business policy.

- auth.ts resolves active staff. commands.ts reloads roles and scopes, authorizes before replay, stores actor/key and request hash, and retries serializable conflicts. CRM and work enforce branch scope and current assignment, with recipient-only pickup notices and revocation on reassignment. Decide object storage only after access/recovery requirements.
- QueueRefresh polls visible pages every twenty seconds. Audit JSON delivery scans, repeated aggregation, report caps, PDF CPU, large exports and sidebar mobile load. Use bounded projections, realistic EXPLAIN plans in staging and grouped KPI queries before adopting new infrastructure.

## Owner-confirmed financial release gates

A financial workflow is complete only when it persists correctly, enforces authorization on the server, leaves an audit trail, supports reversal where appropriate, reconciles to the underlying records, and passes real tests for mobile usability and accessibility.

| Scenario | Required result and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Invoice issued and unpaid | Issued invoice and outstanding receivable persist. Recognized sales and verified collections remain zero. Verify the invoice, KPI, drill-down and period report agree. |
| Payment recorded, awaiting Admin verification | Receipt persists in the verification queue. It contributes zero to verified collections and recognized sales. Verify recognition is denied before verification, including through direct API calls and retries. |
| Admin verifies received funds | Record verifier, time, amount, reference and the linked allocation. Recognize each eligible amount once under the approved policy. Verify concurrent retries and reconcile ledger, dashboard, receivables and export. Partial settlement remains an open policy decision. |
| Verified payment corrected or reversed | Preserve the original and post a linked, authorized correction or reversal with reason and audit history. Recalculate affected collections, recognized sales and receivables. Test the original and reversal periods, including replay. |
| Expense submitted, approved and paid | Keep submitted, approved and paid amounts attributable to their proper states. Expenses contribute zero to sales. Confirm payment separately from approval. |
| Incorrect expense or other financial entry | Use an authorized correction or reversal with the original record retained, a reason and audit history. Verify that direct edits or silent deletion of protected entries fail. |
| Staff acceptance | Run isolated database and API tests for persistence, authorization, idempotency, reconciliation and recovery. Run real desktop and mobile browser journeys with invited test roles for keyboard access, readable feedback, and usable review and reversal flows. |

These are acceptance requirements for implementation and release. This documentation update does not certify the current finance workflows.
